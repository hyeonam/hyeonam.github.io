Contingent Dreams

2021, Collaborative drawings with Robots




Robots are usually considered as tools, devoid of agency and thus creativity. This robotic art performance - Contingent Dreams - explores the generative role of the human artist, algorithm, machine, and medium in art making. In this performance a robot, programmed and assisted by the artist, draws an algorithmic composition in ink. The drawings come to life as the precise paths of the algorithm are translated into bold, enigmatic brush strokes and anomalous drops of ink. The drawings emerge not just from the imagination of the artist, but also from the code of the algorithm, the mechanical motion of the robot, the physics of the brush bristles and ink, and the texture and absorbency of the paper. Each aspect of the performance adds meaningful contingency to the process, resulting in drawings that evoke control and chaos. The artist's intention continually transforms as accidents accumulate into meaning.

Hye Yeon Nam & Brendan Harmon
